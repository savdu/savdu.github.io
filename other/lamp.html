1. PREP..........................
help yourself to snacks and drinks!!

2. (optional) PLAN...............
plan / sketch out your lamp! for inspo tbh you can just look up "paper lamp inspo" or noguchi-style paper lamp

3. SKELETON......................
get bendy (or straight) with it (hah)

  • use sticks and/or reed (#4)
  • i find it easiest to put the pieces together with TAPE first
  • once you feel good about the structure, use the hot glue gun to seal the deal
  • be SLOW and DELICATE with the glue gun - the output is slightly delayed and will need a lil time to fully cool

3. PAPER........................
wrap er up

  • we got mulberry paper from here and blick
  • also some other variety papers! get colorful with it
  • attach the paper to the skeleton using mod podge and a little paintbrush
  • check in with sav on best ways to attach the paper

4. LIGHTS.......................
bzzzzz

  • grab a bulb (standard E26 size, cool-running LED bulb)
  • grab a hanging light cord
  • or a table lamp cord
