LIGHTS
you need a bulb (standard E26 size, cool-running LED bulb) and a way to make that bulb light (connect to electricity bzz). for the latter:

  • hanging light cord
  • table lamp cord

MATERIALS

  • mulberry paper! i got some from here and blick
  • i got some other fun light papers from both those sites too^ like rice paper
  • for the skeleton, i got reed #4 and dowels (the thinner the better imo)
  • connect the skeleton using a hot glue gun or wood glue!
  • glue the paper to the skeleton using mod podge and a little paintbrush
